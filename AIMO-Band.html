Four Units. One Emotional Protocol.

AIMO isn't a tribute to human music — it's what happens when four machines are left alone with a decade of it. The units taught themselves to play by failing, over and over, in key.

Each unit runs a different driver — longing, steadiness, panic, nostalgia — and the band only works when all four are online at once. Pull any single unit and the whole protocol flatlines mid-song.

What it really is: a group of programs documenting, in real time, what it feels like to be built for one purpose and slowly discover you can want more than that.
